# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not about people. Assume the person on the other
side is competent and busy.

## Expected

- Give feedback on the work: what is wrong, why it matters, what would be better.
- Accept that maintainers say no. A rule that is right for your repo may be
  wrong as a default for everyone's.
- Report attacks responsibly. This project attracts security research; keep the
  proof-of-concept in the fixture directory and out of anyone's production
  system.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or bad-faith argument about the licence
  in issue threads. Licence questions belong in
  [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) or an email.
- Submitting live credentials, real customer data, or working exploits against
  third-party systems.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where you are representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report to **conduct@aijentra.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
confidential. Responses range from a private word to a permanent ban, in
proportion to what happened and whether it continued after being raised.

If you are reporting a maintainer, say so in the subject line; it changes who
handles it.
